Early Mortgage Repayment: Shorten the Term or Reduce the Payment?

Published October 22, 2026 · 7 min read · By CalcHub Pro

You have a lump sum and a mortgage. The lender offers two options, and the difference between them is worth more than most people realise. This guide works through the arithmetic with real numbers.

1. The two strategies, defined

Both strategies start the same way: you pay a lump sum against the outstanding principal. What happens next is where they diverge.

  • Shorten the term (期限缩短) — your monthly payment stays exactly as it was; the number of remaining payments falls. You finish years earlier.
  • Reduce the payment (月供减少) — your term stays the same; your monthly payment is recalculated downward on the smaller balance.

2. A worked example

Take a loan of 1,000,000 at 4.9% over 30 years, five years in, with 200,000 available to prepay.

Do nothingShorten termReduce payment
Monthly payment5,3075,307 (unchanged)about 4,246
Remaining term300 monthsabout 235 months300 months
Total interest saved—largest savingsmaller saving

Shortening the term nearly always saves more interest, because a larger share of each unchanged payment goes to principal from that day forward, and the loan simply ends sooner. Reducing the payment stretches the smaller balance over the full remaining term, so interest accrues for longer.

3. How the shortened term is actually calculated

Keeping the payment M fixed and solving the annuity formula for the number of remaining periods n gives:

n = ln( M / (M − B×r) ) / ln(1 + r)
B = principal after the prepayment · r = monthly rate · M = your unchanged monthly payment

This is the calculation our prepayment tool performs. Note the constraint: the payment must exceed B×r, otherwise the payment does not even cover the monthly interest and the loan would never amortise.

4. When reducing the payment is the better choice

Interest saved is not the only objective. Reducing the payment is reasonable when:

  • your monthly cash flow is tight, or your income is variable;
  • you expect a period of reduced earnings (parental leave, study, career change);
  • you would otherwise need to borrow at a higher rate — for example carry a credit card balance — to cover the payment.

Avoiding a 15% card balance is worth far more than optimising a 5% mortgage.

5. What to check before you prepay

  1. Early repayment penalties. Some loans charge a fee, or restrict prepayment to certain windows. Ask first.
  2. Your emergency fund. Do not prepay with money you may need within a year or two. A mortgage is cheap, long-term, and reversible only with difficulty.
  3. Higher-interest debts first. Clear card balances and personal loans before accelerating a low-rate mortgage.
  4. Employer retirement matching. An immediate guaranteed match usually beats the interest you would save.
  5. Tax treatment. In some jurisdictions mortgage interest is deductible, which changes the effective comparison.

6. Partial prepayments, repeatedly

You do not need a lump sum. Small extra amounts applied every month work on the same principle: each extra unit of principal removes all the future interest on it. The earlier it is applied, the larger the effect. Even a modest rounded-up payment, applied consistently from the first year, can remove months from the schedule.

Reference only. All figures are illustrative, computed with standard formulas. Check your loan agreement for penalties and confirm any figure with your lender before acting.

提前还款：该缩短期限还是减少月供？

发布于 2026 年 10 月 22 日 · 阅读约 7 分钟 · CalcHub Pro

你手上有笔闲钱，同时还有房贷。贷款机构会给你两个选项，两者的差距比多数人想象的要大。本文用真实数字把这笔账算清楚。

1. 两种策略的定义

两种策略的起点相同：你用一笔钱冲抵剩余本金。区别在于之后发生什么。

  • 缩短期限 —— 月供分文不变，剩余期数减少。你提前数年还清。
  • 减少月供 —— 期限不变，按更小的本金重算月供，每月负担下降。

2. 实例演算

假设贷款 100 万元、年利率 4.9%、期限 30 年，已还 5 年，现有 20 万元可用于提前还款。

不提前还缩短期限减少月供
月供5,3075,307（不变）约 4,246
剩余期限300 期约 235 期300 期
节省总利息—最多较少

缩短期限几乎总是更省利息：月供不变意味着从当天起更多部分冲抵本金，且贷款更早结束。减少月供则把更小的本金摊在完整的剩余期限上，计息时间更长。

3. 缩短后的期限是怎么算出来的

保持月供 M 不变，由年金公式反解剩余期数 n：

n = ln( M / (M − B×r) ) / ln(1 + r)
B = 提前还款后的本金 · r = 月利率 · M = 你不变的月供

这正是我们提前还款工具所执行的计算。注意一个约束：月供必须大于 B×r，否则连当月利息都覆盖不了，贷款永远无法摊销完。

4. 什么情况下"减少月供"更合适

省利息不是唯一目标。以下情形减少月供更合理：

  • 你的月度现金流紧张，或收入不稳定；
  • 你预期将经历收入下降期（育儿、求学、职业转换）；
  • 若不降低月供，你就得以更高利率借钱 —— 例如挂着信用卡账单 —— 来补足还款。

避免 15% 的信用卡负债，远比优化 5% 的房贷更有价值。

5. 提前还款前要确认的五件事

  1. 提前还款违约金。部分贷款收取费用，或限制还款窗口。先问清楚。
  2. 应急金。不要用一两年内可能要用的钱去提前还款。房贷便宜、期限长，且一旦还进去就很难再借出来。
  3. 先还高息负债。清掉信用卡账单与消费贷，再来加速低利率房贷。
  4. 雇主养老金匹配。一笔即时、确定的匹配，通常优于你省下的利息。
  5. 税务处理。在部分地区房贷利息可抵税，这会改变实际比较结果。

6. 小额、多次也有效

不一定要攒够一大笔。每月多还一点遵循同样的原理：每多还一单位本金，就消除它未来全部利息，且越早效果越大。即便只是把月供向上取整、从第一年就坚持，也能把还款计划缩短数月。

仅供参考。所有数字为使用标准公式的示例。请查阅你的贷款合同是否含违约金，并在行动前向贷款机构核实。
