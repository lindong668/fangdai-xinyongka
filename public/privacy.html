Privacy Policy

LendMath ("we", "us", "the Site") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what information is processed when you use our calculators, why, and what rights you have — including your rights under the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

The short version: all calculations run entirely inside your own browser. We do not receive, store or transmit the loan amounts, interest rates, income figures or card balances you type in. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information we do and do not collect

1.1 Calculator inputs — never transmitted

Every computation is performed client-side in JavaScript. Your loan principal, term, interest rate, income, instalment figures and conversion amounts never leave your device. We have no server-side record of them, and we cannot reconstruct them.

1.2 Information processed automatically

Like almost every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process standard request data needed to deliver pages and keep the service secure, including: IP address, user-agent, requested URL, timestamp and referring page. This is used for security, abuse prevention and reliability — for example to block automated vulnerability scanners.

1.3 Currency conversion requests

When you use the exchange-rate tool, your browser sends a request to a public rate API containing only the currency codes involved (for example USD and CNY). No amount, no identity and no personal data is included. The API operator sees an anonymous request from your IP.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set first-party cookies for tracking. However, third parties that serve advertising on this site may set cookies or similar identifiers.

2.1 Google AdSense and Google advertising cookies

We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure and prevent fraud. This enables personalised advertising where permitted.

  • Manage your ad personalisation: Google Ads Settings
  • Learn how Google uses data: Google advertising technologies
  • Industry-wide opt-out: aboutads.info · Your Online Choices (EU)

2.2 Local storage

We use browser localStorage solely to remember your chosen interface language (English or Chinese). This stays on your device, contains no personal identifiers, and can be cleared at any time in your browser settings.

2.1 Cookie consent (Google Funding Choices)

Under the Google EU User Consent Policy, Google may display its own consent message (Google Funding Choices / the Google consent message) to visitors in regions where consent is required before non-essential cookies are set. Where that message is shown, you may accept or refuse personalised advertising there, and you may change your choice at any time.

You can also manage advertising cookies independently at any time via Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Clearing cookies in your browser resets the choice stored on your device.

3. Legal bases for processing (GDPR Article 6)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for delivering and securing the service and preventing abuse; and consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for advertising cookies and personalised ads, where consent is required by the ePrivacy Directive. You may withdraw consent at any time via the links above or your browser settings.

4. Your rights

4.1 If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the right to: access your personal data; rectify inaccurate data; erase data ("right to be forgotten"); restrict processing; object to processing based on legitimate interests; data portability; and not be subject to solely automated decision-making. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

4.2 If you are a California resident (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond the advertising cookies described in section 2. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

4.3 Exercising your rights

Because we hold no account data and no calculator inputs, in most cases there is simply nothing for us to retrieve or delete. For any request, or if you believe we hold data about you, contact us via the contact page and we will respond within 30 days.

5. International transfers

Our infrastructure and advertising partners may process limited request data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

6. Data retention

Calculator inputs: not retained at all (processed only in your browser). Server request logs: retained only as long as needed for security and operational purposes under our hosting provider's retention practices. Language preference: retained in your browser until you clear it.

7. Children's privacy

This site is a general-purpose financial calculator and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We serve the site over HTTPS, keep dependencies minimal, and restrict what runs on the page. No method of transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the limited data involved.

9. Links to other sites

Our pages may link to external tools and references. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date at the top. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: please use our contact page.
