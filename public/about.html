About CalcHub Pro

CalcHub Pro is a free online calculator site focused on turning the financial decisions people find confusing into plain numbers: what a mortgage actually costs, what a credit card instalment really charges you, and what your money is worth in another currency.

What we offer

Mortgage analysis

More than a monthly payment figure. We calculate the full amortisation, run an interest-rate stress test so you can see what happens if rates rise, estimate how much interest an early repayment would save, and check your debt-to-income ratios against commonly used lending thresholds.

Credit card instalments

instalment plans are usually advertised as a small monthly fee rate — "0.6% per month" sounds cheap. We convert that into a true annual percentage rate (APR) using the internal rate of return of your payment schedule, and compare 3, 6, 12 and 24-month plans side by side, so you can see the real cost.

Currency conversion

Live mid-market rates for major currencies, with the data source and update time clearly labelled, so a "reference rate" is never confused with a bank's quote.

Our principles

  • Transparent formulas. We use standard, published financial formulas — annuity amortisation for equal-instalment loans, IRR for instalment APR. No black boxes.
  • Private by design. Every calculation runs in your browser. Your numbers are never uploaded, stored or shared.
  • Independence. We are not a lender and take no commission from any financial product. Results are reference only, never financial advice.
  • Free and accessible. No registration, no paywall, works on desktop and mobile.

Where our data comes from

Mortgage and credit card results are derived purely from standard financial formulas using the figures you enter. Exchange rates are retrieved from public rate APIs and represent daily mid-market reference rates — not bank buying or selling rates. See our Disclaimer for important limitations.

Guides

We also publish original guides explaining how mortgages, instalment plans and exchange rates actually work, written to be useful years from now rather than to chase headlines.

关于 CalcHub Pro

CalcHub Pro 是一个免费在线计算工具站，专注把"看不懂、算不清"的金融决策变成清清楚楚的数字：房贷到底要付多少、信用卡分期真实收费几何、你的钱换成另一种货币值多少。

我们提供什么

房贷分析

不只是一个月供数字。我们计算完整摊销明细，做利率压力测试（看利率上升会怎样），估算提前还款能省多少利息，并按常用风控口径检查你的负债收入比。

信用卡分期

分期常被宣传为很低的月费率 —— "月费率 0.6%"听起来很便宜。我们按你还款现金流的内部收益率（IRR）把它换算成真实年化利率（APR），并横向对比 3、6、12、24 期，让你看清真实成本。

汇率换算

主流货币的实时中间价，并明确标注数据来源与更新时间，让"参考价"不会被误当成银行报价。

我们的原则

  • 公式透明。一律采用公开的标准金融公式 —— 等额本息用年金摊销法，分期年化用 IRR 反解法。没有黑箱。
  • 隐私优先。所有计算在你的浏览器内完成。你输入的数字不会被上传、存储或分享。
  • 独立中立。我们不是贷款机构，不从任何金融产品收取佣金。结果仅供参考，不构成理财建议。
  • 免费可用。无需注册，无付费墙，桌面与手机皆可使用。

数据来源

房贷与信用卡结果完全由你输入的数字经标准金融公式推导；汇率取自公开汇率接口，属于每日中间参考价，并非银行买入价或卖出价。重要限制请参阅我们的免责声明。
