Before you write

  • We cannot look up your calculation. Everything runs in your browser and is never sent to us, so we have no record of what you entered. Please include your inputs.
  • We cannot tell you whether to take a loan. That is a decision for you and a qualified adviser.
Beware of impersonation. CalcHub Pro is a free calculator site. We will never ask for your bank details, passwords, verification codes, or payment of any kind — not for results, not for "unlocking" features, not ever. If someone claims otherwise, it is not us.
