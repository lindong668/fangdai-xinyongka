Credit Card Instalments: Why "0.6% per Month" Is Really About 13% a Year

Published October 22, 2026 · 8 min read · By CalcHub Pro

"Convert your purchase into 12 easy instalments at just 0.6% per month." It sounds cheap: 0.6 × 12 is 7.2%, which feels manageable. The true cost is closer to 13% a year. This guide explains why, and how to work out the real number yourself.

1. The trick: the fee never shrinks, but your balance does

With a typical instalment plan, the fee each month is calculated on the original amount, not on what you still owe. On a 12,000 purchase at 0.6% per month:

Monthly fee = 12,000 × 0.6% = 72, every single month
Monthly principal = 12,000 ÷ 12 = 1,000
Total monthly payment = 1,072

By month 12 you owe only 1,000, yet you are still paying a 72 fee — a fee of 7.2% on the amount actually outstanding that month. Averaged across the year, you had use of roughly half the money, but you paid a full-year fee on all of it. That is why the effective rate is about double the headline figure.

2. How to get the real rate: IRR

The honest measure is the internal rate of return of the cash flows — the rate that makes the present value of your payments equal the amount you received. Solve for the monthly rate r in:

P = ∑ (payment) / (1 + r)k   for k = 1 to n
then APR ≈ r × 12

For the example above, r works out to about 1.086% per month, giving an APR of about 13.03% — versus the advertised 7.2%.

3. A quick rule of thumb

For a short instalment plan with a constant fee on the original balance, the true annual rate is roughly 2 × monthly fee rate × 12 × n/(n+1), which approaches double the nominal rate as the term lengthens. The rule of thumb to remember: the true APR is close to twice the advertised annual figure.

4. Comparing terms

Using the same example at 0.6% per month, the true APR stays fairly stable across terms but the total fee grows with length:

TermMonthly paymentTotal feeApprox. true APR
3 months4,072216about 10.8%
6 months2,072432about 12.2%
12 months1,072864about 13.0%
24 months5721,728about 13.6%

The monthly payment looks smallest at 24 months, but you pay twice the total fee. If you can afford the shorter term, it is cheaper.

5. When an instalment plan is still reasonable

  • Zero-fee promotions. If there is genuinely no fee and no interest, it is free short-term credit. Read the conditions: some convert to a high rate if you miss a payment.
  • You would otherwise revolve. If the alternative is carrying a balance at 18–20% APR, a 13% instalment is cheaper.
  • Cash flow timing. Spreading a necessary expense can be rational, as long as you price the cost honestly.

6. Traps to watch for

  • Fee charged upfront, non-refundable. Paying early may not refund future fees.
  • Early settlement penalties. Some plans charge the remaining fees if you clear early.
  • Minimum payment illusion. Paying the minimum on a revolving balance can take decades and cost several times the purchase.
  • Fee on the original balance. Always ask whether the fee is on the original amount or the outstanding balance — it changes the real rate substantially.
  • Reduced credit limit. Instalments can consume your available credit.
Reference only. Calculations use the IRR method on a constant-fee schedule. Issuers may calculate and disclose costs differently. Always read your card agreement and confirm figures with your issuer.

信用卡分期：为什么"月费率 0.6%"实际年化接近 13%

发布于 2026 年 10 月 22 日 · 阅读约 8 分钟 · CalcHub Pro

"12 期分期，月费率只要 0.6%。"听起来很便宜：0.6 × 12 = 7.2%，似乎可以接受。但真实成本接近年化 13%。本文解释为什么会这样，以及如何自己算出真实数字。

1. 关键：手续费不缩水，但你的欠款在缩水

典型分期中，每月手续费按原始金额计算，而非按你剩余未还的部分。以 12,000 元、月费率 0.6% 为例：

每月手续费 = 12,000 × 0.6% = 72 元，月月如此
每月本金 = 12,000 ÷ 12 = 1,000 元
每月还款 = 1,072 元

到第 12 个月你只欠 1,000 元，却仍在付 72 元手续费 —— 相当于当月实际占用资金的 7.2%。全年平均下来，你只用了约一半的钱，却按全额付了一整年的费用。这就是为什么实际利率约为宣传数字的两倍。

2. 如何得到真实利率：IRR 法

诚实的衡量方式是现金流的内部收益率（IRR）—— 使你还款现值等于你拿到手金额的那个利率。解出月利率 r：

P = ∑ (每期还款) / (1 + r)k   k 从 1 到 n
则 APR ≈ r × 12

上例中解出 r 约 1.086%/月，对应 APR 约 13.03% —— 而宣传口径是 7.2%。

3. 一个快速估算口诀

对按原始本金恒定收费的短期分期，真实年化约等于 2 × 月费率 × 12 × n/(n+1)，期限越长越接近名义费率的两倍。记住这句口诀：真实 APR 约为宣传年化数字的两倍。

4. 不同期数的横向对比

仍以 12,000 元、月费率 0.6% 为例，真实年化在各期数间相近，但总手续费随期限增长：

期数月供总手续费真实年化约
3 期4,072216约 10.8%
6 期2,072432约 12.2%
12 期1,072864约 13.0%
24 期5721,728约 13.6%

24 期的月供看起来最轻松，但总手续费翻倍。负担得起的话，期数越短越便宜。

5. 什么情况下分期仍然合理

  • 真正的零手续费活动。若确实无息无费，就是免费的短期资金。但要看清条款：部分活动一旦逾期即转为高利率。
  • 否则就得挂账循环。若替代方案是按 18~20% 的循环利率挂着账单，那么 13% 的分期更划算。
  • 现金流时点错配。把一笔必要支出摊开是理性的，只要你诚实地给成本定价。

6. 需要留意的坑

  • 手续费一次性收取、提前还款不退。提前结清未必退还未发生的费用。
  • 提前结清违约金。部分方案提前还清时要收剩余费用。
  • 最低还款的错觉。循环账单只还最低还款额，可能要还几十年，总支出可达消费本金的数倍。
  • 按原始本金收费。一定要问清手续费是按原始金额还是剩余本金计 —— 这会显著改变真实利率。
  • 占用信用额度。分期会吃掉你的可用额度。
仅供参考。本计算采用恒定收费下的 IRR 法。发卡行的计算与披露口径可能不同。请阅读你的信用卡协议并向发卡行核实。
